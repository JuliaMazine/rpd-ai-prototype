# Risk Map

| **ID** | **Risk** | **Category** | **Probability** | **Impact** | **Response** | **Owner** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | Changes to NSU’s RPD requirements require rework and delay MVP completion | Regulations | 1 | 3 | **Mitigate:** Agree on a baseline requirements version, monitor changes, and keep templates configurable.<br>**Contingency:** Assess new requirements with stakeholders and adjust scope or schedule if necessary. | Project manager |
| **R2** | First-year students are unavailable or contribute less time than planned, reducing team capacity and delaying MVP tasks | People | 3 | 3 | **Mitigate:** Confirm student availability early and plan essential MVP tasks around the confirmed core team.<br>**Contingency:** Reassign student tasks to the core team or remove optional features from the MVP. | Project manager |
| **R3** | The local LLM produces inaccurate or incomplete RPD drafts, requiring excessive manual correction | Technology | 4 | 4 | **Mitigate:** Evaluate draft quality early using representative examples, verified source data, and structured templates.<br>**Contingency:** Limit generation to sections that meet quality criteria and provide manual editing for the rest. | LLM developer |
| **R4** | Insufficient labelled course–competency examples prevent the ranking module from achieving acceptable quality | Data | 4 | 3 | **Mitigate:** Audit available labelled examples early and prioritise labelling a small representative dataset.<br>**Contingency:** Use a retrieval-based competency ranking baseline with teacher confirmation instead of supervised training. | Data/ML lead |
| **R5** | Teachers find the system difficult to use or see little benefit over manual RPD preparation, limiting adoption | People | 3 | 3 | **Mitigate:** Involve teachers in early usability tests, provide editable drafts and brief onboarding, and measure time saved.<br>**Contingency:** Simplify the workflow and focus the pilot on tasks where teachers see clear value. | UX / requirements lead |
